Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Star Concrete Solutions LLC ("Star Concrete Solutions," "we," "us") handles information on our website, starcspr.com, and in Star Concrete Books, our internal software tool.

Star Concrete Books

Star Concrete Books is a private, internal tool used only by Star Concrete Solutions LLC. It connects only to our own company's QuickBooks Online account, through Intuit's official API, so that we can view and record our own accounting information, such as invoices, expenses, and payments. It is not offered, sold, or made available to any other business or to the public, and it does not connect to anyone else's QuickBooks account.

The tool accesses only the accounting data in our own QuickBooks company. That data can include our customers' and vendors' names, contact details, and transaction amounts as recorded in our books. We use this data only to run our own bookkeeping. We do not sell it, rent it, or share it with third parties for their marketing. Access credentials are stored securely and are never published. We can revoke the tool's access at any time from our QuickBooks account.

Information collected on this website

When you contact us or request a quote through this website, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your name, phone number, email address, and project details. We use it only to respond to you and to provide our products and services. Like most websites, our web host may automatically record basic technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, for security and site operation.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us operate our business (for example, our website host and Intuit QuickBooks as our accounting system), or when required by law.

Security and retention

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we hold, and we keep it only as long as needed for business, accounting, and legal purposes.
